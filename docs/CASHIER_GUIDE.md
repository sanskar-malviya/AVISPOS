# Cashier guide — Avi's Food Corner POS

## Start of the day
1. Switch on the receipt printer.
2. Open the **Avi's POS** app on the tablet (it may already be open).
3. If the top bar shows a yellow **🖨 Connect printer** button, tap it and choose the printer.

## Taking an order
1. **Tap the food photo** once for each plate. Tap Chole Bhature twice = 2 Chole Bhature.
   The red number on the photo shows how many are in the order.
2. Check the **Current Order** on the right. Use **+** / **−** to change quantities, or **✕** to remove an item.
3. Tap **Continue to Payment →**.

## Taking payment
- **Cash:** tap **Cash**, collect the money, tap **Cash Received — Complete**.
- **UPI:** tap **UPI** and let the customer pay. **Wait until your UPI app or sound box says the payment
  succeeded.** Then tap **Customer Has Paid — Complete → Yes, payment received**.
  ⚠ Never confirm UPI before you have seen the success message.

The receipt prints by itself, then the screen returns to a new order. Tap **New Order** to go back sooner.

## If something goes wrong
| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Printing failed** (red) | The sale is already saved. Check the printer is on and has paper, then tap **Retry Print**. Retrying never charges or records the order twice. |
| Orange **Offline** bar at the top | Keep billing as normal. Orders are saved on the tablet and go to the cloud by themselves when the internet is back. Don't re-enter them. |
| Wrong item added | Tap **−** or **✕** next to it before payment. |
| Customer changes their mind before paying | **Clear Order** → confirm. |
| Need to go back from the payment screen | Tap **← Back to Order**. The items stay. |
| Customer wants a refund or cancellation after paying | Call the owner. Only the owner can cancel or refund. |
| A dish is sold out | Tell the owner (they switch it to unavailable). |

## Good to know
- No typing is needed for normal billing.
- Prices come from the owner's settings. If a price looks wrong, tell the owner.
- **Orders** and **Settings** are for the owner only.
